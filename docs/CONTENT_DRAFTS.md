# Free content drafts for Tradivex

These are drafts for manual review and publication. Verify every provider, tax, regulatory, and market-data statement against the current official source before publishing. Each draft should use a unique slug after checking the existing blog data and sitemap.

## 1. How to Calculate Trading Position Size in Dollars

**Search intent:** US trader wants to calculate shares or contracts from a fixed loss budget.

**Outline:** Define account risk; explain stop distance; show the formula; cover shares, futures ticks, forex pip value, commissions, gaps, and correlated positions; link to `/calculators/position-size` and `/calculators/atr-position-size`.

**Original angle:** Use the same worked example across a stock, a futures contract, and a forex trade, while clearly separating assumptions from provider-specific contract rules.

## 2. Risk–Reward Ratio vs Trading Expectancy: Why Both Matter

**Search intent:** Trader wants to know whether a 2:1 setup is enough.

**Outline:** Explain ratio; calculate break-even win rate; add trading costs; introduce expectancy; show why win rate and average win/loss must be measured together; link to `/calculators/risk-reward`, `/calculators/expectancy-profit-factor`, and `/calculators/net-trading-cost`.

**Original angle:** Include a small sensitivity table showing how fees and slippage change the break-even win rate.

## 3. What Options Probability of Profit Actually Estimates

**Search intent:** US options user wants to understand modelled probability.

**Outline:** Define probability of profit; distinguish it from probability of expiring in the money; explain implied volatility, time, premium, early exercise, assignment, spread, and model risk; link to `/calculators/options-probability` and `/calculators/options-strategy`.

**Original angle:** Show the same option under two volatility assumptions and explain why the estimate changes without calling either result a forecast.

## 4. How to Compare US Trading Platform Costs Properly

**Search intent:** User comparing brokers or platforms wants total cost rather than a headline commission.

**Outline:** Separate commission, spread, exchange/regulatory fees, margin interest, options contract fees, data subscriptions, wire fees, and inactivity rules; explain account and jurisdiction differences; link to `/calculators/net-trading-cost`, `/calculators/brokerage`, and an existing relevant tool page.

**Original angle:** Provide a comparison worksheet readers can fill using each provider's official fee schedule.

## 5. Portfolio Risk: Why Three “Small” Trades Can Still Be One Large Bet

**Search intent:** Trader wants to measure correlated exposure.

**Outline:** Explain position risk versus notional exposure; show correlation clustering; discuss sector, currency, and crypto beta; explain gaps and liquidity; link to `/calculators/portfolio-risk-allocation` and `/calculators/drawdown-recovery`.

**Original angle:** Use a transparent three-position example and list what the simplified calculator does not model.
