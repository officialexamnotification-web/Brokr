## Quora answer draft

**Question:** How do I calculate the correct position size for a trade?

Start with the maximum amount you are willing to lose, not with the number of shares you want to buy. Multiply account equity by your chosen risk percentage, measure the distance between entry and stop, and divide the risk budget by the per-share, pip, point, or tick risk. Then check commissions, spread, slippage, contract size, currency conversion, and whether the instrument can gap through the stop. A calculator can show the arithmetic, but it cannot guarantee execution at the stop price. Tradivex has a free input-driven example here: https://www.tradivex.com/calculators/position-size
